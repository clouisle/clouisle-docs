A team is Clouisle's resource isolation boundary: Agents, workflows, knowledge bases, tools, and Skills all belong to a team. Every operation inside a team must pass two layers of checks: **global role permissions** and the **team member role**.

This page covers creating teams, adding/changing/removing members, leaving, transferring ownership, and deleting a team, plus what each step shows when it fails. For the full role model see [Roles & Permissions](./roles-permissions.mdx) and the [permissions and roles reference](../../../reference/permissions.mdx).

## Creating a Team

<Callout type="info">
  Creating a team requires team management permission in the Admin area. The entry point is **Admin** → **Teams**. Ordinary members cannot see it.
</Callout>

1. Go to **Admin** → **Teams**.
2. Click **Create Team** and fill in:
   - **Team Name** (required, globally unique; a duplicate reports that the team name already exists)
   - **Description** (optional)
   - **Team Avatar** (optional, uploaded from your machine; JPEG/PNG/GIF/WebP/SVG/ICO, 200×200 recommended, 10 MB limit)
3. Save.

The creator automatically becomes the team **owner**.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Not implemented / Roadmap**: specifying another owner at creation, adding initial members, invite/join settings, and resource quotas are not part of the creation flow. Members join afterwards through **Add Member**; ownership moves through **Transfer Ownership**.
</Callout>

After creation, select the team in the team switcher. When creating Agents, workflows, or knowledge bases, confirm the current team — otherwise the resource lands in the wrong team and can only be corrected by recreating it.

## The Two Team Views

The team switcher offers two entry points with completely different permissions and capabilities:

| Entry | Location | Requires | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Manage all teams (system admin)** | **Admin** → **Teams** (`/teams`) | Team viewing permission in the Admin area | List every team (paged, searchable by name/description), create, edit, delete, bulk delete |
| **Manage current team** | `/app/team` | Membership in the team; the entry link only appears for Super Admins or team owners/admins | Member management, editing team basics, leaving, transferring ownership, viewing authorized models (read-only), and centrally editing tool approval policies |

The **Model Authorization** tab on `/app/team` is **read-only**: it shows the models authorized for the team, their enable state, and today's usage/limits, and notes that a system administrator assigns them. Every write operation on model authorization requires a Super Admin (see below).

## Team Details and Members

### Team Overview

The `/app/team` header shows the team avatar, name, a **default team** badge, and a badge for **your role in this team**. The Members tab lists avatar, username, email, and role, and filters locally by username/email.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Not implemented / Roadmap**: team details do not show resource quotas (Agent/workflow/storage limits) or usage activity (conversation counts, run counts, API calls), and there are no team-level notification settings or separate resource tabs.
</Callout>

### Model Authorization

Model authorization is **Super Admin only**: team members can only view it. Authorizing, bulk authorizing, changing quotas, and revoking are done by an administrator in **Admin** → **Teams**, or reviewed (read-only) on the **Model Authorization** tab under **Manage current team**.

Configurable values include daily/monthly token limits, daily/monthly request limits, enable state, and priority. Granting or revoking sends an in-app notification to the team. See [Managing Team Models](../models/manage-models.mdx) for details.

### Tool Approvals

The **Tool Approvals** tab lists built-in, custom, MCP, and shared tools visible to the team, with the current policy for each:

- **Allow**, **Deny**, or **Require caller confirmation**.
- MCP tools can add overrides by exact operation name (for example `tool:search`); unmatched operations inherit the default decision.
- A private tool can only be edited by its creator; shared tools are managed by the owning team and are read-only here; viewers see no save action.

This tab changes **each tool's own policy**; it does not create team, site, entry-point, or workflow approval layers. Approval decisions and policy changes are written to the audit log. See [Tool Approvals and Caller Confirmation](../tools/tool-approvals.mdx) for the full rules.

## Adding Members

<Callout type="info">
  Requires team management permission and that you are **Owner** or **Team Admin** in this team. Ordinary and read-only members cannot see the add button, and forced attempts are rejected.
</Callout>

1. Open the **Members** tab of `/app/team` and click **Add Member** (shown only when you have management capability).
2. Enter the target user's **full username or email** in "Username or email".
3. Choose a role: **Admin**, **Member**, or **Viewer** (**Owner** is not offered).
4. Confirm.

Matching and validation:

| Case | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Exact username match, or case-insensitive exact email match | User found |
| No match | Reports that the user does not exist — there is **no fuzzy search or picker** |
| Only a fragment (e.g. the prefix `john`) | No match; the same "user does not exist" message |
| The user is already a member | Reports that they are already a team member |
| Attempting to choose Owner | Not allowed; Add Member cannot assign Owner |
| Choosing Admin while the target lacks global team management permission | Operation rejected |

<Callout type="error" title="Grant the global role before team Admin">
  Adding a user who lacks global team management permission as a team **Admin** is rejected outright. The order must be: first give them the **Team Admin** role (or any role that includes team management) under **Admin** → **Users**, then set them to **Admin** in the team.
</Callout>

After a successful add: the new member receives a team notification and the team receives a summary notification; membership takes effect **immediately** and there is no invite/accept flow.

## Changing a Member's Role

<Callout type="info">
  Only the team **Owner** (or a Super Admin) can change roles. A team **Admin** cannot change member roles.
</Callout>

1. Find the member in the list and open the **...** menu.
2. Choose **Change Role** and select **Admin** / **Member** / **Viewer**.
3. Save.

Validation rules:

| Case | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Caller is not Owner (or Super Admin) | Operation rejected |
| Target member is currently Owner | The Owner's role cannot be changed; use Transfer Ownership |
| New role is Owner | Not allowed; promoting to owner requires Transfer Ownership |
| New role is Admin while the target lacks global team management permission | Operation rejected |
| Target is not a team member | Reports that the member does not exist |

A successful change notifies the target user.

<Callout type="warn">
  Role changes are **not written to the audit log**: team auditing covers creating/updating/deleting teams, adding/removing members, and team model authorization only.
</Callout>

**The interface also hides buttons**: you cannot act on yourself, and a team Admin cannot see the menus for other Admins and the Owner.

## Removing Members

| Scenario | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Removing someone else | Requires team management permission and Owner/Admin; the menu is also subject to the "cannot act on another Admin/Owner" rule above |
| Removing yourself | Any member can do this; it is equivalent to leaving the team |

Shared limits: **the Owner can never be removed** (including by themselves); a target who is not a member reports that the member does not exist.

After removal: the user immediately loses access to all team resources; only the membership is deleted — **their conversations, API keys, and personal resources are not deleted** (conversations become inaccessible because team access is gone). Both the team and the removed user receive notifications. They can be added again later.

## Leaving a Team

1. `/app/team` → **Settings** tab → danger zone, click **Leave Team**.
2. Confirm in the dialog.

<Callout type="error">
  **An owner cannot leave**: the danger zone says "As the team owner, you cannot leave the team directly — transfer ownership first." You must transfer ownership first. The **default team** also hides the leave button — that is interface behavior, not a permission issue. Changing the default team is done by an administrator in site settings.
</Callout>

After leaving you lose access to the team's resources; existing conversations are kept. The team list refreshes and the page returns to `/app`.

## Transferring Ownership

<Callout type="info">
  Requires team management permission and that you are **Owner** in this team (or a Super Admin). A team Admin does not see this menu item.
</Callout>

1. Open the member list at `/app/team`, find the target member, then **...** → **Transfer Ownership** (only the Owner sees this item).
2. Confirm in the dialog.

Rules (applied in order):

1. The target must be a team member, otherwise it reports that the member does not exist.
2. The target must **already hold global team management permission**, otherwise the operation is rejected. Likewise, grant the global role first, then transfer.
3. The target is the current Owner → operation rejected.
4. The original Owner's member role is **downgraded to Admin**, the target becomes **Owner**.
5. The old and new Owner each receive a notification.

<Callout type="warn">
  The transfer is immediate and irreversible: the former Owner keeps only Admin rights and **cannot change it back** (changing roles requires Owner). Confirm the target before proceeding.
</Callout>

## Editing Team Information

Requires team management permission and that you are Owner/Admin in the team.

- `/app/team` → **Settings** tab: change the team name, description, and avatar (inputs are disabled when validation fails).
- The edit dialog in **Admin** → **Teams**: the same fields, requiring team edit permission in the Admin area.

Fields and constraints: the name is required and globally unique (a duplicate reports that the team name already exists); the description is optional; the avatar is written by the upload component. There is **no** team slug, alias, or domain field.

## Deleting a Team

<Callout type="error">
  Deleting a team is irreversible and destructive, and can only be done in **Admin** → **Teams**. A team Owner has **no** delete entry under `/app/team`.
</Callout>

1. In the team list, open the target team's **...** → **Delete Team** (not shown for the default team).
2. Confirm in the dialog — it only asks for confirmation, you do **not** type the team name.
3. The default team is rejected: the default team cannot be deleted.

Deletion impact:

| Deleted | Kept |
| --- | --- |
| The team record and all member relationships | Members' user accounts |
| The team's Agents, workflows, knowledge bases, tools, Skills, assets | Audit logs (compliance) |
| Team model authorizations, tool configuration, notifications, import sessions | Members' account data (API keys belong to the user, not the team) |
| Agent conversations and their messages (cascading with the Agent) | — |

## Team Roles and Permissions

Team roles are four fixed values — **Owner** / **Admin** / **Member** / **Viewer** — and **carry no permission list of their own**; they act only as team gates. Effective capability is the intersection of global role permissions and the team gate.

### Gate Rules

- Team management actions (editing team info, managing members, deleting tools, and so on) require both the corresponding global permission **and** Owner/Admin in the team.
- A team **Viewer** can only view and execute (chat, run workflows, search knowledge bases, execute tools and Skills); every other write is rejected.
- Super Admins bypass membership and team gates entirely.

### Team Management Permissions

| Operation | Owner | Team Admin | Member | Viewer |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| View the team and members | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Edit team info (name/description/avatar) | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Add members | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Change member roles | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Remove members | ✓ | ✓ (not other Admins/Owner) | ✗ | ✗ |
| Leave the team | ✗ (transfer first) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Transfer ownership | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Delete the team | ✗ (Admin area) | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |

### In-Team Resource Permissions

| Operation | Owner | Team Admin | Member | Viewer |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Create Agents / workflows / knowledge bases | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Edit **others'** Agents / workflows / knowledge bases | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Delete Agents, publish Agents and workflows | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Chat with Agents, run workflows | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Create/edit your own tools and Skills | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Edit **others'** tools / Skills | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Delete tools | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Search/test knowledge bases, upload/rename/delete documents | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | Read-only search |
| Execute tools and Skills | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

<Callout type="warn" title="API keys are not part of a team">
  API keys belong to the individual: ordinary users see only their own keys, and team Owners/Admins cannot see or deactivate someone else's keys. Team member roles have **no effect** on API keys.
</Callout>

### Default Team Role

Site settings can specify a **default team** and a **default member role**. The default role accepts only **Viewer / Member / Admin** (any other value falls back to Member, which is also the default). It applies when users register or are auto-created through SSO; existing memberships are not overwritten. **Owner is never assigned automatically.**

### Custom Team Roles

> **Not implemented.** Teams have only the four fixed roles Owner, Admin, Member, and Viewer, each mapping to a fixed set of gate rules; you cannot add or remove permissions per team. For finer-grained permissions, use global custom roles (see [Roles & Permissions](./roles-permissions.mdx)).

## Collaboration and Notifications

Team collaboration happens through shared resources: Agents, workflows, knowledge bases, tools, and Skills are shared within the team, and members access them according to their role gate. Workflow run history is visible to team members (subject to permissions), including who ran what and when.

Automatic team notifications (delivery channels are configured by an administrator in site settings — see [Notifications and External Channels](./notifications.mdx)):

| Event | Trigger | Recipients |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member joined | Adding a member | The new member + the team |
| Member removed | Removing a member | The removed user + the team |
| Role changed | Changing a member role | The affected user |
| Ownership transferred | Transferring ownership | One each to the old and new Owner |
| Model granted / revoked | Granting or revoking a model | The team |

> **Not implemented / Roadmap**: no per-team notification configuration, @mentions, shared conversations, team activity feed, document comments, or in-team messaging.

## Troubleshooting

### Cannot Access Team Resources

1. Confirm the team switcher on the left has the correct team selected.
2. Confirm you are a member of the team (being able to open `/app/team` means you are).
3. Confirm your global role has the required capability; a team Viewer can only read and execute.
4. Check whether the resource is **private**: private resources are invisible to non-creators and access is denied.

### Cannot Change a Role / No "Change Role" Menu

- Only the **Owner** (or a Super Admin) can change roles.
- The interface also hides options: you cannot act on yourself, and a team Admin cannot see the menus for other Admins and the Owner.
- The Owner's role cannot be changed; to replace the owner, transfer ownership.
- To promote someone to team Admin, they must already hold global team management permission.

### Adding a Member Reports "User Does Not Exist"

You must enter the **full username** or **full email** (email is case-insensitive). Fragments, nicknames, and display names do not match — the system performs no fuzzy search.

### Adding a Member Is Rejected

The Admin role was chosen but the target lacks global team management permission. Give them the Team Admin global role first, then set them as team Admin.

### Transfer Ownership Is Rejected

- You are not the Owner (or a Super Admin).
- The target is not a team member — add them first.
- The target lacks global team management permission.
- The target is already the Owner.

### Cannot Leave the Team / No Leave Button

The Owner cannot leave, and the default team hides the button. Transfer ownership first; for the default team, an administrator must change the default team in site settings.

### Cannot Delete the Team

- The entry exists only in **Admin** → **Teams** and requires team delete permission in the Admin area.
- The default team cannot be deleted.

### Permissions Did Not Change After a Role Change

The frontend permission snapshot cached in the browser may be stale: refresh the page or sign in again. The backend evaluates every request live, so no cache expiry is involved.
