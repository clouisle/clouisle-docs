Clouisle's authentication flow may include password, email verification, administrator approval, CAPTCHA, password changes, TOTP, and SSO. Site settings determine which steps are enabled.

## Sign-up & Email Verification

Clouisle supports self-registration when enabled by administrators.

### Self-Registration

1. Navigate to the login page at `https://your-domain.com` and click **Sign up**.
2. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Username**: Unique identifier, max 50 characters
   - **Email**: Valid email address
   - **Password**: At least 8 characters (see [Password Requirements](#password-requirements))
   - **Confirm Password**: Re-enter password
3. If human verification is shown, click **Click to verify you are human** to complete it.
4. Click **Register** to submit.
5. If email verification is enabled, check your email for the verification code or link.
6. Verify your email to activate your account, then log in.

<Callout type="info">
  The first registered user is automatically promoted to Super Admin. If administrator approval is required (`require_approval`), the account enters a pending-approval state and cannot log in until approved. Administrators receive a global notification.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note">
  There is no invitation-based registration flow. Users are created via self-registration, by administrators (user management), or automatically through SSO (when `sso_auto_create_users` is enabled).
</Callout>

## Login Methods

### Password Login

1. Navigate to the login page and enter your **username** or **email**.
2. Enter your **password**.
3. If human verification is shown, click **Click to verify you are human** to complete it.
4. Click **Log In**.

### SSO Single Sign-On

If your organization has configured SSO, you can log in with corporate credentials.

1. Navigate to the login page and click the SSO provider button (e.g., "Continue with Google", "Continue with GitHub").
2. Authenticate with your SSO provider.
3. You are redirected back to Clouisle and logged in automatically.

**First-time SSO login:**

- If your email matches an existing account, the SSO connection is linked to it (email matching is enabled by default)
- If no account exists, a new account is created automatically (if `sso_auto_create_users` is enabled)

**Supported SSO providers:**

- OAuth2/OIDC (Google, GitHub, GitLab, etc.)
- SAML 2.0 (Azure AD, Okta, OneLogin)
- CAS (University systems)

See [SSO Configuration](./sso.mdx) for details.

### First-Time Login

After your first successful login:

1. **Welcome Screen**: A welcome message is displayed
2. **Profile Setup**: Complete your profile information (optional)
3. **Team Assignment**: You may be assigned to the default team (if configured) or added to a team by an administrator
4. **Dashboard Access**:
   - Regular users see the platform interface
   - Administrators see the admin dashboard

## Login Protection

### Human Verification

Login and self-registration may require a click-through CAPTCHA. The CAPTCHA proof is valid for `300` seconds (5 minutes) by default; re-fetch it after a failure or expiration.

### Account Lockout

Repeated failures trigger account lockout and rate locking:

- Default 5 failed attempts before lockout
- Lockout lasts 15 minutes (configurable)
- Contact your administrator if locked out repeatedly

### Login Anomaly Detection

The system tracks your usual login locations and devices, and sends security notifications when logging in from a new location. Check your notifications if you see anomaly alerts.

## Password Management

### Password Requirements

| Requirement | Default |
| --- | --- |
| **Minimum Length** | 8 characters (configurable) |
| **Uppercase** | At least one uppercase letter (A-Z) |
| **Number** | At least one digit (0-9) |
| **Special Character** | Not required by default |
| **No Recent Reuse** | Cannot reuse the last 5 passwords (configurable) |

<Callout type="note">
  There is no dictionary / weak-password check.
</Callout>

**Example of a valid password:**
```
MySecure#Pass2026!
```

**Examples of weak passwords:**
```
❌ 12345678       (no letters/uppercase)
❌ abcdefgh       (no numbers)
❌ Pass123        (too short)
```

### Changing Your Password

1. Open **Profile Settings** (profile menu) → **Account** tab
2. Find the **Change Password** section
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Current Password**: Your existing password
   - **New Password**: Your new password
   - **Confirm New Password**: Re-enter new password
4. Click **Change Password**
5. You'll see a success message

As you type, the UI shows a strength indicator (weak / fair / good / strong).

**Tips for strong passwords:**
- Use a mix of character types
- Make it at least 12 characters long
- Avoid personal information (name, birthday)
- Consider using a password manager

### Resetting a Forgotten Password

1. Go to the login page and click the **Forgot Password?** link
2. Enter your **email address**
3. Click **Send Reset Link**
4. Check your email inbox

The email contains:
- Subject: "Password Reset Request - Clouisle"
- Reset link with a verification code (valid for **10 minutes**)
- Instructions

5. Click the reset link / enter the code from the email
6. Enter your **new password** and confirm it
7. Click **Reset Password**
8. You'll see a success message; log in with your new password

<Callout type="warn">
  Reset links/codes expire after 10 minutes. Resend is rate-limited to 60 seconds.
</Callout>

### Password Expiration

If your administrator enables password expiration:

- Password expires after a configured number of days (e.g., 90)
- You'll receive reminders before expiration (default: warning starts 7 days before)
- After expiration, you must change your password to log in

**Changing an expired password:**

1. Try to log in
2. You'll see a "Password expired" / "Change password required" message
3. Enter your current password to authenticate first, then enter a new password
4. Log in with the new password

## TOTP Two-Factor Authentication

If your organization enables 2FA, you must enter a TOTP code when logging in.

### Setting Up TOTP

1. Open **Profile Settings** → **Account** tab and click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** (or follow the administrator-enforced wizard)
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.), or enter the secret manually
3. Enter the 6-digit verification code to confirm
4. Download or copy the one-time backup codes; each backup code can only be used once
5. Click **Enable**

### Logging In with TOTP

1. Enter your username and password
2. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app
3. Click **Verify**

### Backup Codes

- Save backup codes in a safe place
- Use a backup code if you lose access to your authenticator
- Each code can only be used once

## Session Management

### Session Duration

- Sessions last until the configured **session timeout** (`session_timeout_days`, in days, default 30)
- There is no "Remember Me" option and no inactivity-based logout

### Single Session Mode

If enabled by your administrator, only one active session is allowed per user — logging in elsewhere invalidates the previous session.

### Logging Out

1. Click your profile icon in the top-right corner
2. Select **Logout**
3. You are redirected to the login page

Logging out only invalidates the current session/token.

## Account Security

### Security Notifications

You'll receive notifications for:

- Password changed successfully
- Failed login attempts / account locked
- Login from a new location/device
- Password expiration reminders

**Check notifications regularly** to detect unauthorized access.

### If Your Account Is Compromised

If you suspect unauthorized access:

1. **Change your password immediately**
2. **Enable 2FA** (if not already enabled)
3. **Review login notifications** for suspicious activity
4. **Contact your administrator** to report the incident

### Admin Password Reset

An administrator can reset your password from user management:

1. Administrator resets your password
2. You receive an email with a temporary password
3. Log in with the temporary password
4. Change your password

## Troubleshooting

### Cannot Access Login Page

1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify the URL is correct
3. Try a different browser
4. Clear browser cache and cookies
5. Contact your IT administrator

### Forgot Password

1. Click **Forgot Password?** on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for the reset link
4. Click the link and enter a new password
5. Log in with your new password

See [Password Management](#password-management) for details.

### Account Locked

1. Wait for automatic unlock (default 15 minutes)
2. Contact administrator for immediate unlock
3. Use password reset if you forgot your password

### Email Verification Not Received

1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Wait a few minutes (email may be delayed)
3. Click **Resend Verification Email**
4. Verify the email address is correct
5. Contact administrator if still not received

### SSO Login Fails

1. Verify you're using the correct SSO provider
2. Check if your SSO account is active
3. Clear browser cookies and try again
4. Contact your SSO administrator
5. Try password login if available

See [SSO Configuration](./sso.mdx) for SSO-specific troubleshooting.

### Account Pending Approval

- Your account requires administrator approval
- Wait for an administrator to activate your account
- You'll receive an email when approved
- Contact administrator if waiting too long

### Session Expired

1. Log in again
2. Contact administrator to adjust the session timeout
3. Check if you're logged in on another device (single session mode)

## Best Practices

### Security Recommendations

**✅ Do:**
- Use a strong, unique password
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Log out when using shared computers
- Keep your email address up to date
- Review login notifications regularly
- Use SSO if available (more secure)

**❌ Don't:**
- Share your password with others
- Use the same password as other services
- Stay logged in on public computers
- Ignore login anomaly notifications

### Account Management

**Regular Tasks:**
- Update your password when required
- Review login notifications for suspicious activity
- Keep profile information current
